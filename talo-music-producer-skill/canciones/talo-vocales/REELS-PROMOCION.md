## REEL 1: "¡BUSCA LA A!" (15 segundos)

### PROMPT IMAGEN INICIAL (Nano Banana 2):
```
Talo with binoculars in jungle, excited expression, looking for bee (abeja),
colorful jungle background with trees, vines, flowers,
bright colors - greens, yellows, oranges, reds,
adventure mood, child-friendly, Bluey-style illustration,
text: "¿Dónde está la A?"
```

### PROMPT ANIMACIÓN KLING 2.5:
```
Duration: 15 seconds. Format: 9:16 vertical video.
Starting image: Talo excited with binoculars in colorful jungle.

Detailed animation:
[0-3s] Talo slowly raises binoculars to eyes, head tilts upward searching
[3-6s] Camera zoom/pan through binoculars (POV effect), shows jungle canopy
[6-9s] Golden bee (Abeja) flies into frame from left, wing flapping smooth
[9-12s] Text "¡A! ¡A! ¡A!" appears center screen, glowing, letters bounce to beat
[12-15s] Talo jumps celebrating with arms raised high, big smile, confetti/sparkles

Motion specifications:
- Binoculars raise smoothly (2s duration)
- POV zoom through binoculars shows depth, jungle details
- Bee movement: realistic wing flapping, gliding motion
- Text pulse synchronized with music rhythm
- Jump is bouncy, energetic, lands and holds
- All movements flow with upbeat music

Visual effects required:
- Golden glow around bee (halo effect)
- Sparkles/stars around "¡A!" text, pulsing
- Light rays through jungle canopy (God rays effect)
- Smooth color transitions between scenes
- No abrupt cuts, all transitions smooth

Color palette: Greens (jungle) 60%, yellows/golds (bee) 20%, browns 20%
Music sync: Jump lands on beat
Mood: Playful, exciting discovery
Final frame: Hold Talo celebrating for 1s before fade
```

**Caption:**
```
¿DÓNDE ESTÁ LA A? 🐝✨
¡Sigue a Talo en la selva!
Descubre todas las vocales con "La Selva y Las Vocales"
#TaloVocales #VocalA #CancionesParaNiños
```

## ESPECIFICACIONES KLING 2.5

**Formato:** 9:16 (vertical)  
**Duración:** 15-30 segundos  
**Calidad:** HD 1080x1920  
**Frame rate:** 24fps  
**Codec:** H.264

**Workflow:**
1. Generar imagen inicial con Nano Banana 2
2. Copiar imagen + prompt detallado a Kling 2.5
3. Exportar MP4 (ya tiene audio sync integrado)

**Prompts están optimizados para:**
- Sin necesidad de multi-imagen
- Descripción ultra-detallada de movimientos
- Timing específico para cada acción
- Sincronización con música
- Efectos visuales claros
