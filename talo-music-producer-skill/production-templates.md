## Matriz: Negative Guidance por Tema

Usa la negación específica del tema para mejorar precisión de Mureka y evitar desviaciones.

| Tema | Avoid (Negative Guidance) |
|---|---|
| **Números (1–10)** | Avoid: rhyming counting, sing-songy delivery, skipping numbers, counting backwards without context, adult educational tone, overly abstract visual imagery, unclear pronunciation of numbers |
| **Colores** | Avoid: mixing colors without clear separation, listing colors monotonously, abstract color theory, monochrome production, unclear color names, images that don't match color names, confusion between similar colors (red/pink) |
| **Letras / Alfabeto** | Avoid: spelling pronunciation (A-B-C vs "a bee see" confusion), skipping letters, song focused only on letter names without context, overcomplicated alphabet order, mixing uppercase/lowercase confusion, regional letter pronunciation |
| **Animales** | Avoid: realistic animal sounds that are too loud or scary, predator-prey relationships, endangered species without context, onomatopeias that are unclear, animal behaviors that are violent or inappropriate, mixing wild and domestic animals without differentiation |
| **Emociones** | Avoid: adult emotional complexity, dismissing feelings, forced happiness, traumatic or heavy emotional scenarios, mixing emotions without resolution, lack of validation for child feelings, abstract emotional concepts |
| **Rutinas (comer, bañarse, dormir)** | Avoid: scary scenarios (bathing), rushing through steps, skipping safety steps, adult language about bodies, negative tone about routines, overly silly tone that undermines the lesson, incomplete routine cycles |
| **Cuerpo / Partes del cuerpo** | Avoid: medical/clinical language, anatomical complexity, mixing body parts without context, inappropriate focus, adult health concerns, ambiguous body part names, gender-specific assumptions |
| **Movimiento / Acciones** | Avoid: movements that are unsafe or too complex for age, static delivery, overly abstract movements, unclear action instructions, movements that conflict with the song tempo, movements that require objects not mentioned |
| **Familia / Relaciones** | Avoid: assumptions about family structure, emotional manipulation, complex relationship dynamics, adult themes, dismissing family diversity, guilt or shame about relationships, unclear relationship roles |
| **Comida** | Avoid: junk food promotion without healthy balance, allergic triggers (nuts, dairy) prominence, overly realistic chewing sounds, food waste messaging, adult diet language, cultural insensitivity about food, pressure to eat |
| **Transporte** | Avoid: accident imagery, scary vehicle sounds, overly realistic engine sounds, vehicle-specific jargon, mixing transportation types confusingly, traffic safety without positive framing, adult traffic concerns |
| **Naturaleza / Clima** | Avoid: scary weather (storms, floods), overwhelming nature imagery, overly realistic animal predator-prey, pesticide/farming references, confusing seasonal transitions, adult environmental anxiety |
| **Formas** | Avoid: abstract geometry without visual clarity, confusing shape names, mixing 2D and 3D without distinction, overly mathematical language, static shape presentation, unclear shape examples |
| **Comportamiento / Buenos hábitos** | Avoid: shame or guilt language, punishment framing, overly authoritarian tone, conflicting messages, adult behavior standards, unclear expectations, negative peer pressure |
| **Inglés básico** | Avoid: mixing Spanish grammar rules with English, unclear English pronunciation, overly complex English words, cultural English idioms without translation, adult English topics, Latin-centric English (not global) |

## Checklists Micro (para usar mientras escribes)

### Checklist: Antes de enviar a Mureka

- [ ] Lyrics en español latino neutro (sin modismos regionales)
- [ ] Cada línea tiene máx 6–7 palabras
- [ ] Coro aparece antes de segundo 20
- [ ] Hook es claro y memorable (primeros versos)
- [ ] [Pausa] tags especificados con duración (mínimo 2 seg)
- [ ] Style Prompt en inglés (no español)
- [ ] Negative Guidance incluido y específico del tema
- [ ] Production Notes con BPM, duración, claridad vocal
- [ ] Metatags correctamente formateados: [Intro], [Verse 1], [Chorus], etc.
- [ ] Talo activo en cada sección (sujeto de acciones, no pasivo)

### Checklist: Después de generar en Mureka

- [ ] Voz es clara y comprensible
- [ ] BPM coincide con el solicitado
- [ ] Coro es memorable y diferente del verso
- [ ] Instrumentos coinciden con lo solicitado
- [ ] Pausa de 2 segundos se respeta
- [ ] Outro cierra bien (sin corte abrupto)
- [ ] Timing total está en rango (1:30–2:15)
- [ ] Edad objetivo es evidente en complejidad

## Notas Importantes

- Toda plantilla está lista; cambia **solo lo que necesites** dentro de `[CORCHETES]`
- Si una canción falla, usa el **Feedback Loop Rápido** para documentar y re-intentar
- Registra todo aprendizaje nuevo en **feedback-learning-loop.md** para mejorar futuras canciones
- Estas plantillas están optimizadas para Mureka v2.0+; si usas versión anterior, ajusta Style Prompt según especificaciones anteriores
- Para Shorts/Reels, usa la **SHORT VERSION** y adapta BPM al rango 115–135
